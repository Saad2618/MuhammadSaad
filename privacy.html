Privacy

This is the personal professional site of Muhammad Saad, a senior full stack developer in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. It is a static website. It has no database and no user accounts.

What you send

If you email, call, message on WhatsApp, or use LinkedIn, you are contacting Muhammad Saad through that service. Those companies process the message under their own policies once it leaves this site.

The contact form collects your name, email, optional company, project type, budget range, timeline, project description, and preferred contact method. A hidden field is included only to catch automated spam. Do not fill it.

When the form is not connected

Until a form endpoint is set in assets/js/site-config.js, the form does not transmit your message anywhere. It tells you that, and offers an email link instead. Nothing is stored by this website.

When the form is connected

The intended provider is Formspree. Submissions are sent from your browser to the endpoint you configure, then delivered to Muhammad Saad's email. The form identifier is public. No secret API key is placed in this site's JavaScript. Formspree's retention and processing terms apply to messages they receive. You can read them at formspree.io.

Theme preference

The site stores your light or dark theme choice in localStorage on your device. That value is not sent to a server.

Fonts and analytics

Inter and IBM Plex Mono are self-hosted. The site does not request Google Fonts. No analytics script is loaded. If analytics are added later, this page should be updated before that script is enabled. The switch lives in site-config.js as analytics.enabled and is not wired to a provider.

Deletion

To ask for a message you sent to be deleted, email msaaddeveloper1@gmail.com. Muhammad Saad can delete it from his inbox. If the form provider still holds a copy, deletion there follows that provider's process.
