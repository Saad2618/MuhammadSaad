    Process

    A clear path from first call to release.

    The sequence stays visible, so you always know whether the work is being defined, built, reviewed, or released.

    1. 01

      Discovery

      Goals, users, constraints, and the systems that already exist. This is where scope gets honest.

    2. 02

      Planning and estimation

      Milestones, risks, and a written estimate tied to agreed deliverables.

    3. 03

      Development

      Short build cycles with software you can review, not a long silence followed by a surprise.

    4. 04

      Testing and review

      Functional checks, fixes, and a walkthrough before anything is called done.

    5. 05

      Deployment and support

      Release, handover notes, and a support window agreed before launch day.

    Pricing

    Three ways to engage.

    Pick the commercial shape that matches the work. Figures are published here only after they are finalized.

    Hourly development

    Rate available on request

    Best for open-ended support, team augmentation, and work that will change week to week.

    • Time tracked against agreed priorities
    • Useful when the backlog is still moving
    • You can pause or extend without rewriting a fixed scope

    Fixed-price projects

    Rate available on request

    Best when the scope, integrations, and finish line can be written down.

    • A defined set of deliverables
    • Milestones you can review
    • Change requests stay explicit

    Monthly retainer

    Rate available on request

    Best for maintenance and a reserved amount of senior time each month.

    • Ongoing improvements after launch
    • A predictable response for production issues
    • Capacity reserved in advance

    The final estimate depends on scope, complexity, integrations, and agreed deliverables. A written number follows discovery. It is not implied by the cards above.
